# International Management — True or False

Endless static study quiz based on the supplied Doh, Luthans & Gaur 12th-edition Chapter 1–3 slide decks and Chapter 1–4 review screenshots.

- 80 concepts, each with one true and one false statement (160 authored variants).
- Each pass covers every concept once, with equal true/false answers, shuffled independently. A new pass starts automatically; its first concept cannot repeat the previous one.
- No question totals, timer, score, or final exam screen.
- Short Russian and English explanations with chapter/slide or discussion references.
- Keyboard: T / F to answer, Enter to continue; standard Tab/button navigation also works.
- Historical brand questions are explicitly framed as course-key examples. Screenshot item 4 is omitted because its company is unnamed.
- Chapter 4 definitions were checked against The Culture Factor, Trompenaars Hampden-Turner and GLOBE (links in the site’s materials section). Open-ended discussion topics were converted into limited factual/conceptual statements, not graded opinions.

Open index.html locally. No dependencies or build required. Keep app.js, questions.js and style.css alongside it.

Commits to main deploy automatically to https://international-management-test-1.vercel.app via Vercel. Framework: Other; output: repository root.
